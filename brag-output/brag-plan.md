# Brag Plan: English for Iraq (1st Intermediate)

## What is this app?
An authentic, interactive web platform for the Iraqi English curriculum (1st Intermediate / الأول متوسط), featuring full listening tracks, comic book story readers with American voiceover, and rigorous Activity Book exercise validation.

## The angle
Iraqi English textbooks just came to life: from the crowded streets of New York with Ahmed & Faisal to the swift bird rescue, students can listen, read, and solve real Activity Book exercises with smart handwriting normalization.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on sleek Iraqi curriculum emerald & slate palette:
"منهج الأول متوسط... صار تفاعلي!"
A dramatic reveal of the interactive lesson cards with sound waves.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1 (0:03 - 0:08):** Ahmed & Faisal in New York — dynamic comic scenes with authentic American voiceover and word highlighting.
- **Moment 2 (0:08 - 0:13):** Authentic Activity Book Practice — pure English open-ended fill-in-the-blank lines, no cheating multiple-choice buttons, with instant Arabic feedback and model answers.
- **Moment 3 (0:13 - 0:16):** Story Time: "My new friend, the swift" — interactive story elements and vocabulary flashcards.

## Outro / punchline (0:16 - 0:20)
- "تعلم، استمع، وتدرب على الامتحانات المدرسية بكل سهولة"
- Logo reveal: **English for Iraq – Unit 1**
